Why this cat

A loaf, a vest, and a bridge.

He found a sunbeam and cancelled the rest of the day.

Crumple is an original cat, not a studio character. Apricot fur, cream muzzle, every paw tucked. The sitting is the personality.

The vest was already on. The button has never been even.

He treats the crooked button as tailoring. Unfolding, in his view, is a rumor started by people with somewhere to be.

He needed a bridge, not a pair of shoes.

pons is a wallet-signed launchpad on Robinhood Chain. A launch mints a fixed supply to a bonding curve. If that curve is bought out, the pool graduates into Uniswap v4 and the liquidity is locked. pons does not take custody of the funds.

$CRUMP is the loaf that crossed by staying put.

The joke is short enough to post. The token details are not a joke. Match the network and the full contract before anyone signs.

Field guide

How to find him on pons.

The story is silly. The click path should be boring and exact.

01

Bring your own wallet

Use a self-custody wallet that can add a custom network. Keep a little ETH on Robinhood Chain for gas. pons never holds the tokens or the funds.

02

Add the chain from the docs

Robinhood Chain is chain ID 4663. Confirm the RPC in the pons docs before you save it. The docs currently publish https://rpc.mainnet.chain.robinhood.com.

03

Open the launch, not a lookalike

Go to the pons launchpad. Ignore any site that only shouts the ticker. The market that counts is the one whose contract matches this page.

04

Wait for the address

Until the ledger prints a contract, $CRUMP is not for sale here. When it does, paste that exact address. Search-by-name is how the wrong cat gets bought.

05

Read it, then sign

Check the network, the token you receive, the amount, and the fee. The signature happens in your wallet. Meme tokens can go to zero.
